- **Standard template**: "Greetings!" + verbose capability listing (Session 55)
- **Epistemic template**: Content + "I can't verify from my internal state" (Session 57)

**Implication**: **Template collapse is orthogonal to mode**. It's a separate phenomenon.

**Revised framework**:
- **Mode dimension**: Standard vs Epistemic (epistemic stance)
- **Expression dimension**: Varied vs Template (linguistic repetition)
